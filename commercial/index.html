Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

Restaurants and food-service businesses have climate demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms must stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units have to replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Bend HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, proper tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

Comfort and air balance are critical issues for any food-service operator. A kitchen running negative pressure pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags outdoor air through every door, and makes staff miserable during peak service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and airflow your operation depends on, with written service records that demonstrate a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors. We provide detailed reports and service logs after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

Replacement and maintenance plans are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits the hours you're open. When a job can't happen during service, we do it after close, keeping your kitchen cooking and the dining room comfortable throughout.

Safety

Professional Installation and Documentation

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Bend HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Emergency Service

Fast Commercial HVAC Service

Each hour without climate control costs your business money. In a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. On a retail floor, it empties the aisles on a summer afternoon. In a server room, it risks overheated equipment, and in a leased building it invites tenant disputes. Bend HVAC offers fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties across Bend and surrounding areas, dispatching an experienced technician quickly when you call.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: our technicians have seen them all on commercial equipment. Our first priority is diagnosing the fault and getting heating or cooling back fast. After that, we set up maintenance and monitoring to prevent a repeat, and every response is fully documented for your records.
