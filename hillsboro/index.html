How Hillsboro Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

Many mid-season breakdowns trace back to skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Change your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and starting to struggle, such as climbing utility bills, rooms that never feel comfortable, or a service call every season, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and much easier on you than an emergency one in the middle of a cold snap.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor ignored for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
